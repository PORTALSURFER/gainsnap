# v0.1.7

## Features

- Replaced the preset list with a Normalize toggle: click N to start Peak matching at 0 dBFS, then click again to stop and hold the audible gain.

## Improvements

- New instances remember your last Peak/RMS selection across DAW restarts. Saved projects keep their own mode.

- Match now waits at least one second for repeated signal and a stable measurement before adjusting, preventing boosts from isolated hits followed by silence.

- Automatic gain changes are limited to 24 dB per second in either direction for smoother matching.

## Bug fixes

- Stopping Match during a correction now holds the gain you are currently hearing.
