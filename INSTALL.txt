GainSnap — macOS Apple Silicon VST3

1. Quit your DAW.
2. Copy GainSnap.vst3 into:
   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   In Finder, choose Go > Go to Folder and paste that path.
   Create the VST3 folder if it does not exist.
3. Remove older GainSnap VST3 copies from your plug-in folders to avoid duplicates.
4. Open your DAW and rescan VST3 plug-ins, then load GainSnap on an audio track.

Choose Peak or RMS and set the target. Turn on Match to listen and adjust,
then turn it off to hold the correction. Restart begins a new measurement.
Drag either triangle or the gain knob to adjust it. Drag the numeric fields
vertically, or click and type a value. Shift gives finer numeric-field steps.
Space passes through to the host transport.

This package contains the Apple Silicon macOS build only.
GainSnap is free with optional donations; no license activation is needed.
